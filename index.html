English

Grid Flicker ("the game") is developed by starkdev2201. This policy explains what data the game and the services it uses process.

On your device

  • Scores, statistics, saved games and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.
  • The game does not ask for your name, email, account, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone.

Services we use

  • Google AdMob shows ads (optional rewarded video to continue a game, and occasional full-screen ads between games). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions, to deliver, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. How Google uses data.
  • Google Firebase Analytics measures how the game is used (for example, games started and finished, modes played, session length) using an app-instance identifier and, where allowed, the advertising ID. Events never include your name or contact data.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports and diagnostic information (device model, OS version, the error) to fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config lets us adjust game settings (such as ad frequency limits) without an update.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland (and wherever required by law), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads and related data use. You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy → Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • To request deletion of data linked to your app installation, contact us at the email below.

Other

  • Sharing: tapping Share sends only the image and text you see, through the app you choose.
  • Children: the game is not directed to children under 13.
  • Retention: analytics data is kept for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days.
  • Changes: we will update this page before any change in how data is used.

Contact: pulsegrid.studio@gmail.com

O AdMob pode coletar o ID de publicidade do aparelho, a localização aproximada obtida pelo IP e informações de interação com anúncios, para exibir, medir e personalizar anúncios e evitar fraudes. Como o Google usa dados.

  • Firebase Remote Config permite ajustar configurações do jogo (como limites de frequência de anúncios) sem atualização.
